Game Quiz manual

A trivia game for your Twitch chat. The bot asks a video game question, viewers answer with !answer, and points, teams, records and Discord posts run for you.

10 minDefault session length
30 secDefault time per question
100 ptsScore that wins early
+5 +3 -1Correct, close, wrong
  1. Check every trigger in Setup points to the same action.
  2. Open the dashboard and set your session length, points and number of teams.
  3. Press Start, or type !quiz in chat.
  4. Viewers answer with !answer and join a team with !team.

Setup

One C# script runs everything. It talks to the overlay and dashboard over Streamer.bot's WebSocket server and saves its data in your Documents folder.

Question suggestions

Viewers whisper ideas to FallenOneArt, the streamer account, so chat stays clean. A !suggest typed in public chat gets a whisper explaining how.

Whisper this to FallenOneArt: /w FallenOneArt !suggest Your question? | The answer

A viewer named Sam wants to add a question. Sam whispers FallenOneArt (not public chat), with a | between the question and the answer.

Sam whispers FallenOneArt!suggest Which game features the Master Sword? | Zelda
FallenPool_Bot whispers backThanks! Your question was sent for review.
Sam tries again right away!suggest What is Kratos' son called? | Atreus
FallenPool_Bot whispers backslow down a little, you can suggest again in 42s.
Sam forgets the | divider!suggest Who is Master Chief?
FallenPool_Bot whispers backsend it like this: !suggest Your question? | The answer

The first suggestion now waits on the dashboard's Ideas tab as: Sam asks "Which game features the Master Sword?" with the answer "Zelda". You can add "The Legend of Zelda" as an extra accepted answer, then press approve. The bot says in chat: "@Sam's question was approved and added to the quiz!"

No links, and nothing starting with ! or /. Questions you already have are turned away.

Ideas wait on the dashboard's Ideas tab. Edit the question, answer and extra answers, then approve or reject. Approving adds it to the quiz and thanks the viewer. You can open or close the box any time, and the bot announces it.

How a session runs

It ends when the clock runs out, someone hits the target score, you press Stop, or the questions run out. Then the podium shows, records are checked and the recap posts.

1

You press Start. The bot announces the session length.

2

A random unused question goes to chat and the overlay.

3

Viewers answer with !answer. Wrong guesses are allowed.

4

With 15 seconds left, a time warning shows.

5

A correct answer, time-out, skip or reveal closes it.

6

After 5 seconds the next question appears.

Scoring and answers

+5Correct. Matches the answer or an extra accepted answer.
+3Close enough. Part of the answer (over 3 letters). Question stays open.
-1Wrong. Anything else.

What counts as correct

  • Capitals, punctuation, accents and a leading "The" are ignored.
  • Small typos and missing or extra spaces are forgiven. Short answers are strict, longer ones allow more.
  • Numbers must match, so "Resident Evil 4" is never accepted for "Resident Evil 5".
  • Add extra accepted answers per question (like "MGS") in the dashboard's "Also accept" box.

Teams

Run 2 to 8 teams. Viewers type !team and do not choose.

How placement works

  • The smallest team gets the viewer. Tied teams: one is picked at random.
  • The bot whispers "You were placed on (team)!".
  • Typing !team again only tells them their team. They are never moved.
  • With team mode off, they are told to play for themselves.

Dashboard

Everything you can control without touching chat.

  • Start / Stop: start (saving settings first) or end a session.
  • Skip: drop the question and move on. Reveal: show the answer and close it.
  • Settings: session 30 to 7200 sec, question 10 to 180 sec, target score, next-question delay 1 to 30 sec, points for correct, close and wrong.
  • Award points: give or take points by viewer name. Reset scores: clear this session.
  • Add, edit or remove questions. The same question text updates its answer.
  • Extra accepted answers are separated by |.
  • Upload an answer picture (PNG or JPG, up to 5 MB). It is saved under the answer's name for the overlay.
  • Overlay theme, timer style and timer colors (solid or gradient).
  • Show or hide the leaderboard panel. Pick the podium style and test it.
  • Sound on or off, volume 0 to 100.
  • Number of teams (2 to 8), team names, team mode on or off, team reset.
  • Leaderboard view: Hunters, Teams, or rotate.
  • "Thanks for playing" line (up to 120 characters), the stream-ended card and a test version.
  • Export settings to a named file and import one later from the list.

Discord posts

Paste a webhook link into the dashboard. Turn Enabled on for automatic posts. "Send now" buttons work even when it is off.

At session end

Podium picture, recap, and a winner card with avatar, points, lifetime wins, accuracy and records held.

On events

New records and monthly champions.

On demand

All-time top 10, the month's leader, and the stream-ended card with winner, winning team and hardest question.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happens when viewers type !answer

A question must be open. After a correct answer or time-out, answers are ignored until the next question appears.

!rank, !teamname or !suggest does nothing

Each needs its own command trigger on the same action. Suggestions also need the Whispers trigger.

The dashboard looks empty

Make sure the Streamer.bot WebSocket server is running, then reload. The dashboard asks for the current state when it connects.

Teams reset to 2 after restarting Streamer.bot

Open the dashboard. It re-sends the team count and names when it connects.

Discord says it is not a webhook link

Copy the full link again from Discord's channel settings. It starts with https://discord.com/api/webhooks/.

Whispers fail

Viewers whisper FallenOneArt, not the bot account. Twitch limits whispers, and the viewer needs a verified account.
