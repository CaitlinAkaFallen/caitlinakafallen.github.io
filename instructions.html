Spotify Setup Guide

FallenOneArt · Music Player

Connect your Spotify account to the FallenOneArt Music Player overlay. This uses PKCE authentication — no backend server, no secrets stored anywhere but your own browser.

01

Create Your Spotify App

  1. 1 Go to the Spotify Developer Portal.
  2. 2 Sign in with your Spotify account.
  3. 3 Click Create App and fill in the required information.
  4. 4 Once created, you'll see your Client ID and Client Secret — copy the Client ID for later.
  5. 5 Add your Redirect URI to the app's settings in the portal.

03

Link Your App to the Dashboard

  1. 1 Open the Music Player Dashboard and go to the Connection tab (first tab in the sidebar).
  2. 2 Paste your Client ID into the Spotify Client ID field. It saves to this browser automatically, even if the login gets interrupted partway through. Use the eye icon next to the field to double-check what you pasted before connecting.
  3. 3 Confirm the Redirect URI shown on the dashboard matches exactly what you added in the Spotify Developer Portal in Step 2. If it doesn't match, Spotify will reject the login.
  4. 4 Click Connect Spotify. You'll be sent to Spotify to approve access, then redirected right back to the dashboard.
  5. 5 The status LED turns teal and shows "Connected as [your name]" once everything's linked. If it says connection expired, just reconnect — your Client ID is already saved.
  6. 6 Copy the OBS Browser URL from the same tab and add it as a Browser Source in OBS if you want a dockable control panel — separate from this dashboard, which is only for you to control settings.
  7. 7 Copy the Music Player URL from the same tab and add it as a Browser Source in OBS. That's the overlay itself — separate from this dashboard, which is only for you to control settings.
Connection tab — pasting your Client ID and confirming status
✓

Once your app is saved, grab only the Client ID — never the Client Secret — and paste it into the Connection tab of the Music Player Dashboard to link everything up.

!

Nothing happening after you click Connect? The most common cause is a Redirect URI mismatch between the dashboard and your Spotify app settings — even a missing trailing slash will block it. Double-check both match character-for-character.
